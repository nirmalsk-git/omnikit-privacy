OmniKit privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

OmniKit ("the app") is built to work without an account and without sending your data anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit or sell personal data, usage analytics or crash reports.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter (notes, tasks, reminders, health logs, vehicle records, scanned documents, vault items and files you process) is stored only on your device. Vault items are encrypted with a key derived from your PIN and protected by your device's secure hardware.

Optional cloud sync

If you turn on sync (part of Pro), the app keeps a copy of your data in a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive account. Vault items stay encrypted with your PIN; the Vault is left out unless you choose "Include Vault". We never see this data; Google's or Microsoft's privacy policy governs that storage. You can stop syncing from Settings at any time, and remove the app's folder by disconnecting OmniKit in your Google or Microsoft account.

Permissions

Camera (document and QR scanning, torch), notifications and exact alarms (alarms and reminders), approximate location (compass true north only; never stored or transmitted), files you choose (PDF, image, audio and file tools), biometrics (unlocking the Vault), local Wi-Fi (offline file sharing between your own devices on the same network; nothing passes through the internet). Text recognition and document scanning run on the device. Each permission is requested only when you first use the feature.

Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed by Google Play or the Microsoft Store. We receive no payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Export or back up all data from Settings → Sync, backup & restore. Uninstalling removes everything on the device.
